FAQs

Common Questions

Do you make eggless cakes?

Yes, all our popular flavours are available in 100% eggless versions at the same price, with no compromise on taste or texture.

How much advance notice do you need for a custom cake?

For simple designs we can often manage same-day. For detailed theme or fondant cakes, we recommend ordering at least 24–48 hours in advance. Call us to confirm availability.

Do you deliver in Kalher and Bhiwandi Rd?

Yes, we offer delivery across Kalher and nearby areas on Bhiwandi Rd, along with takeaway from our shop.

Can I send a reference photo for a custom design?

Absolutely — share your reference photo with us on WhatsApp or in person, and we'll do our best to recreate the design.

What are your shop hours?

We're open daily from 9:00 AM to 11:30 PM. Call ahead if you're placing a large or urgent order.
